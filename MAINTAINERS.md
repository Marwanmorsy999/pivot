## Becoming a Maintainer

We welcome contributors who want to take on more responsibility. To become a maintainer:

1. **Consistent Contributions**: Regularly contribute quality PRs over 3+ months
2. **Community Engagement**: Help others through issues and discussions
3. **Domain Expertise**: Develop deep knowledge in one or more areas of the codebase
4. **Express Interest**: Let the core team know you're interested in taking on more responsibility

Contact @Marwanmorsy999 to discuss becoming a maintainer.
